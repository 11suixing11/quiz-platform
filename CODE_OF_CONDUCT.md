# Code of Conduct

Know Yourself should be a respectful place to collaborate on self-reflection tools.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Respect that users may approach self-reflection topics with different backgrounds.
- Avoid medical or clinical certainty unless backed by appropriate professional context.
- Keep issues and PRs focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Posting private mental-health details about another person.
- Encouraging users to replace professional care with quiz results.
- Repeated off-topic or bad-faith comments.

Maintainers may close issues, hide comments, or block users when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
